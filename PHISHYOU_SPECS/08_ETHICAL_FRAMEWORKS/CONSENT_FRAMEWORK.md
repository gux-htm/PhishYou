## 3. Consent Withdrawal & Pause Protocol

```
WITHDRAWAL TRIGGERS:
- Employee sends "STOP" or "OPT OUT"
- Employee contacts security manager requesting withdrawal
- PhishYou detects high psychological distress (Tier B/C)
- Organization admin requests withdrawal

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (upon withdrawal):
1. All pending messages HALT immediately (within 5 minutes)
2. No further communications sent to that employee
3. Employee receives confirmation email
4. Incident logged in audit trail
5. Debrief scheduled within 24 hours

COOL-OFF PERIOD:
- Employee cannot be re-engaged in campaigns for 30 days minimum
- Organization must document reason for engagement
- Employee consent re-obtained before any future campaigns
```

### Debrief Template (Security Manager Conducted)

```

                    SECURITY DEBRIEF SESSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

EMPLOYEE: [Name]
DATE: [Date]
SECURITY_MANAGER: [Name]

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
AGENDA:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. WELCOME & REASSURANCE (5 min)
   - "This is NOT a test you passed or failed"
   - "This is TRAINING designed to help you"
   - "We appreciate your participation"

2. ATTACK SUMMARY (10 min)
   - What attacks you faced
   - How many turns/interactions
   - Which channels were used

3. WHAT HAPPENED & WHY (10 min)
   - If you fell for the attack: "Here's how it was designed to work"
   - If you defended: "Here's why your defense worked well"
   - Common vulnerabilities: authority, urgency, fear

4. LEARNING POINTS (10 min)
   - Key lesson from this attack
   - How to recognize similar attacks in real life
   - Verification procedures to remember
   - Who to contact if unsure

5. COACHING & NEXT STEPS (5 min)
   - Recommended training modules
   - Resources for further learning
   - No further action needed unless desired

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DO:
✓ Praise the employee for participating
✓ Frame as learning opportunity, not failure
✓ Focus on the attack techniques used
✓ Provide specific, actionable advice
✓ Ask if they have questions/concerns

✗ Shame or ridicule the employee
✗ Use results for disciplinary purposes
✗ Compare to other employees

## 5. Exemption Categories

### Who Can Be Exempted?

```
MEDICAL EXEMPTIONS:
- Employees with diagnosed anxiety disorders (documented)
- Employees with recent trauma (HR documented)
- Employees under medical care for mental health
- Pregnancy-related stress (if requested)

OCCUPATIONAL EXEMPTIONS:
- C-suite executives (CISO discretion)
- Employees in crisis/emergency response
- New employees (first 30 days)
- Employees on medical leave

LEGAL EXEMPTIONS:
- Employees with active legal proceedings
- Whistleblowers or protected reporters
- Employees with known vulnerabilities to manipulation

TEMPORARY EXEMPTIONS:
- High-stress periods (EOY close-out, major project)
- Recent significant life events
- Active medication adjustments

PROCESS:
1. Employee or HR submits exemption request (with reason)
2. CISO reviews and approves/denies
3. If approved: Employee excluded from that campaign
4. If denied: Employee informed; can appeal to HR
5. All exemptions logged in audit trail
```
